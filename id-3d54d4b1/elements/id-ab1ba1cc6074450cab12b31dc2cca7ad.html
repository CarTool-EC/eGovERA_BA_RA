Financial Management ()
Definition: The Financial Management Application Service refers to a software service that facilitates the management and processing of financial operations and transactions within an organization. It encompasses a range of functionalities, including budgeting, accounting, financial planning, reporting, invoicing, and payment processing. The service streamlines financial processes, ensures accurate recording and tracking of financial data, and provides insights into the organization's financial performance. It may integrate with other systems such as Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, banking platforms, and payment gateways to enable seamless financial operations Source: International Journal of Public Administration and Management Research (IJPAMR) Source reference: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/331892729_The_Role_of_Financial_Management_Information_Systems_in_the_Public_Sector LOST view: TVA-Functional Architecture Principles Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/egovera/FinancialManagementApplicationInterface EIRA traceability: eira:WebServiceApplicationInterface ABB name: egovera:FinancialManagementApplicationInterface EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock Last modification: 2022-01-18
